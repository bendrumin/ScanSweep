Your kids grabbed your phone. Now your camera roll has 87 photos of the carpet, a thumb, and the dog's left ear — all beautifully out of focus.

SnapSweep finds them in seconds, so you don't have to scroll for an hour.

HOW IT WORKS
• Scan — SnapSweep analyzes every photo right on your iPhone, scoring sharpness and exposure and using Apple's on-device intelligence to spot accidental shots.
• Review — flagged photos appear in one grid, sorted worst-first, with a badge explaining why each one was flagged. A strictness slider lets you tune how picky it is, and you can preview any photo full screen before deciding.
• Clean up — delete the junk in one pass, or file it into a "SnapSweep Flagged" album to deal with later.

BUILT-IN SAFETY NETS
• Nothing is deleted without the iOS system confirmation.
• Everything you delete stays in Recently Deleted for 30 days, so you can always change your mind.
• Prefer zero risk? Use "To Album" and nothing gets deleted at all.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• All analysis happens on your device.
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no network calls. Your photos never leave your phone.

SnapSweep is for every parent whose photo library doubles as a toddler's art project.

SnapSweep finds the junk in your camera roll — the blurry shots, the ones taken in a
pocket, the too-dark and blown-out frames, and the bursts where the same photo was
taken eight times in a row. Everything is analyzed on your device; no photos are
uploaded and there is no account.

Flagged photos appear in one grid with a badge explaining each flag. You choose what
happens: delete them (they sit in Recently Deleted for 30 days), or file them to a
"SnapSweep Flagged" album without deleting anything.

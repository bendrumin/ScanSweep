SnapSweep needs full Photo Library access because it scans the whole library for
low-quality and duplicate photos, and carries out the deletions and album changes the
user explicitly confirms.

No account or sign-in is required. To exercise the app on a review device: open it,
tap "Scan My Library", grant photo access, and the results grid appears when the scan
finishes. Any photo library works; a library containing some blurry or repeated shots
shows the most.

All analysis is on-device (Core Graphics and the Vision framework). The app makes no
network requests and collects no data. Deletions go through the standard PHPhotoLibrary
confirmation sheet and remain recoverable in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

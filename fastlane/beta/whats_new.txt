NEW IN THIS BUILD: near-duplicate detection.

If someone holds the shutter down, you get a dozen near-identical shots. Those are
sharp and well exposed, so the blur and brightness checks never caught them. This
build groups them, keeps the sharpest frame, and flags the rest as "Repeat shot".

Worth trying:
• Run it on your largest library — that is where repeats show up.
• Check the repeat groups. The sharpest of each group is kept and never flagged;
  the results screen says so above the grid. Tell us if it ever picks a worse frame.
• Drag the Strictness slider. It now tunes duplicate matching too.
• The scanning screen and the dashboard were both redesigned.

Known limits:
• Bursts spread over more than three minutes are not grouped yet.
• Very dark or blown-out frames are deliberately excluded from duplicate matching —
  they have too little detail to compare safely — so they are only caught by the
  brightness checks.

Please report anything flagged that you would have kept. False flags matter more to
us than missed junk. Deleting is always your call and stays undoable for 30 days.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

SnapSweep is designed so that your photos and your data never leave your device.

What we collect

Nothing. SnapSweep collects no data of any kind.

  • Photos — SnapSweep asks for access to your photo library solely to analyze photos on your device and to carry out the deletions and album changes you explicitly request. Photo analysis happens entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device frameworks. No photo, thumbnail, score, or any information derived from your library is ever transmitted anywhere.
  • No accounts — SnapSweep has no sign-up, login, or user identifiers.
  • No network use — the app makes no network requests. It contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporters, and no third-party code that phones home.

Deletions

Photos are only ever deleted through Apple's system confirmation dialog, and anything you delete remains in your Photos app's Recently Deleted album for 30 days, where you can restore it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated text will be published at this same URL with a new "last updated" date.

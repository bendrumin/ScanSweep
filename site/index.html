Support & FAQ

Does SnapSweep upload my photos anywhere?

No. Analysis runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple's frameworks. SnapSweep makes no network requests at all — see the privacy policy.

What if it flags a photo I want to keep?

Tap any photo to unselect it (or touch and hold to preview it full screen first). Nothing is ever deleted without your confirmation, and anything deleted stays in your Recently Deleted album for 30 days.

Why does it need full photo library access?

To scan your whole library and carry out the deletions and album changes you request. You can grant limited access instead — SnapSweep will simply scan only the photos you've shared.

Something's not working — how do I get help?

Open an issue on GitHub and we'll take a look. Include your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Still stuck? Reach out via GitHub issues.
